# Changelog

The version shown in RimWorld's mod list comes from `<modVersion>` in `About/About.xml`.

## 0.0.3

+ Combat logs redesigned to better categorise and showcase the events.
+ Can now filter event type per Pawn.
+ Pawns will now reflect any incapable skills
* All family is now recorded, rather than just immediate family.
* Fight tales (wounded, downed, killed etc) now count as Fights instead of Tales, in the viewer and in the Lore tab.
* Deaths name who or what did it, even when the pawn bled out hours later.
* Death entries list what happened, using the game's own combat text for the wounds.
* Fight tales say who did what: "Alex was wounded by a hare", "Alex killed a hare with a revolver".
* Fixed causes like "blood loss, after: Alex died". Older entries are tidied up in the viewer.

## 0.0.2

+ Causes of death for pawns and animals including the condition that killed them (blood loss, plague) and/or the last blow from the combat log.
+ Causes of death shown on Memorial cards and pawn pages in the viewer.
+ Version number shown in the mod list, mod settings, the Lore tab and the viewer.
* The game's death letter is merged into Rimlore's own entry instead of appearing separately spamming entries.

## 0.0.1

+ First release on the Steam Workshop.
